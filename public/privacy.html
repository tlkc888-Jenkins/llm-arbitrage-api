Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

TL;DR: We collect minimal data (request logs for rate limiting and abuse prevention). We don't sell your data. We don't require accounts for basic tools.

1. What We Collect

When you use our API, we may collect:

  • Request data: IP address (hashed), user agent, timestamp, endpoint called
  • Tool arguments: Parameters you send to tools (e.g., location for weather)
  • Usage statistics: Which tools are used, how often, error rates

2. What We Don't Collect

  • Personal identification (no accounts required for free tier)
  • Cookies or tracking across sites
  • Sensitive personal data

3. How We Use Data

We use collected data to:

  • Enforce rate limits and prevent abuse
  • Improve our services and fix bugs
  • Understand which tools are most useful
  • Detect and prevent malicious activity

4. Data Retention

  • Request logs: 30 days
  • Aggregated statistics: Indefinitely (anonymized)
  • Memory tool data: Until you delete it or 90 days of inactivity

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: Hosting (Render), database (Supabase) — under confidentiality agreements
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

6. Third-Party Tools

Some of our tools fetch data from third-party APIs:

  • Weather: wttr.in
  • Geocoding: OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  • Crypto prices: CoinGecko
  • IP location: ip-api.com

When you use these tools, your request parameters (like location) are sent to these services. Review their privacy policies for more information.

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all API traffic
  • IP address hashing in logs
  • Regular security reviews

However, no system is 100% secure. Don't send sensitive data through our tools.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your data
  • Object to processing

Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Check this page for the latest version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@autropic.com
