## Blog Post Ideas

### 1. "The Complete Guide to LLM API Pricing in 2026"
**Target:** "LLM pricing 2026", "LLM API pricing comparison"

**Outline:**
- Introduction: The pricing chaos (800+ models, 1000x price differences)
- Pricing models explained (per token, per request, subscription)
- Provider comparison tables
  - OpenAI pricing breakdown
  - Anthropic pricing breakdown
  - Google pricing breakdown
  - Open source hosted (Groq, Together, etc.)
- Hidden costs (rate limits, minimums, overages)
- How to calculate your actual costs
- When to use cheap vs expensive models
- Conclusion: Introduce SavvyLLM as solution

**Length:** 3,000+ words (comprehensive guide)

### 2. "GPT-4 Alternatives: 10 Models That Cost 90% Less"

- Best for coding: DeepSeek Coder, CodeLlama
- Best for chat: Claude Haiku, GPT-3.5 Turbo
- Best for speed: Groq-hosted models
- When to actually use GPT-4
- How to automatically pick the right model (→ SavvyLLM)

**Length:** 2,000 words
