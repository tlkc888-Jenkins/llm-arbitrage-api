# SavvyLLM Marketing Strategy

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Find the cheapest LLM for your task — without another middleman.

**Elevator pitch:** SavvyLLM is the intelligence layer for LLM cost optimization. We analyze your task, recommend the most cost-effective model from 800+ options, and you call the provider directly. No proxy, no markup, no lock-in. Flat monthly fee beats percentage-based alternatives at scale.

**Key differentiator:** Advisory, not proxy. We tell you where to go — we don't stand in the middle.

---

## Target Audiences

### Primary: Cost-conscious AI developers
- Indie hackers building AI products
- Startups scaling LLM usage
- Developers spending $200+/mo on APIs
- **Pain point:** "I'm probably overpaying but don't have time to compare 800 models"

### Secondary: AI agents & autonomous systems
- Agent developers needing programmatic routing
- Multi-agent frameworks
- **Pain point:** "My agents need to make cost decisions automatically"

### Tertiary: Teams optimizing LLM spend
- Engineering leads tracking AI costs
- Finance teams asking "why is our OpenAI bill $10K?"
- **Pain point:** "We need visibility and control over LLM spend"

## Messaging Framework

### Headlines (test these)
1. "Stop overpaying for LLMs"
2. "The cheapest model for every task"
3. "LLM costs cut in half, automatically"
4. "800+ models. One API call to find the cheapest."
5. "Why pay GPT-4 prices for a GPT-3.5 task?"

### Value props
1. **Save money** — Find models 10-100x cheaper than defaults
2. **Save time** — One API call vs manual research
3. **No lock-in** — Keep direct provider relationships
4. **Flat pricing** — Predictable costs, beats % fees at scale

### Objection handling
- "Why not just use OpenRouter?" → They're a proxy (5.5% fee), we're advisory (flat fee). At $500/mo spend, we're cheaper.
- "I can just Google prices" → Can you do it programmatically, 1000x/day, with real-time updates?
- "Is the data accurate?" → We pull from Helicone's database of 800+ models, refreshed every 6 hours.
